what min. is, what it does, and how to get it

min. is an AI assistant you reach by text message.

There is no app to install. You message it the way you message a friend, and it messages you back.

min. is a personal AI assistant made by Siege AI, Inc. It lives in your messages: SMS, Telegram, WhatsApp, or Discord. Once you connect your calendar and email, it keeps up with your days, remembers the people in them, and takes care of the small things so you don’t have to.

What it does

  • Answers what you ask. Directions, a recipe, what to buy, what a news story actually said, how to phrase an awkward email. It looks things up and gives you a straight answer.
  • Keeps up with your calendar. It knows what is on today, notices when a meeting moves, and reminds you before the things that matter.
  • Reads your inbox so you don’t have to. Connect Gmail and it surfaces the flight confirmation, the lease renewal, the reply you forgot to send, and can draft a reply when you ask.
  • Remembers the people in your life. Who your mom is, when she lands, that your friend just started a new job. It keeps track so you can just talk.
  • Checks in when something matters. The morning of a big day, or after it, to ask how it went. Occasionally, not constantly.
  • Helps with the errand. Ordering food, finding the restaurant’s website, booking the thing. Where it can, it does it. Where it can’t, it hands you the link.
