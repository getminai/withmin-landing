How to sign up

There are exactly two ways to opt in, and you choose one of them yourself.

Option 1 — the form on life.withmin.ai/sms.

  1. Open life.withmin.ai/sms.
  2. Enter your own mobile phone number in the field.
  3. Read the consent text and tick the checkbox beside it. It is unchecked by default, and the button stays disabled until you tick it yourself.
  4. Press “Text me a code”. min. texts a 6-digit code to that number.
  5. Type the code back into the form. That proves the number is yours, and you are opted in.

Option 2 — text min. first. Send any message to +1 (415) 629-3650 from your own phone. Starting the conversation is itself the opt-in, and min. replies to that number and no other.

Consent is one-to-one

  • You give consent for your own number only. There is no way to submit someone else’s number and have min. text them.
  • Consent is never shared, sold, rented, or forwarded to any third party, affiliate, or partner, and no third party can opt you in on our behalf.
  • Consent is never implied or bundled. Ticking the box is a separate, affirmative act; it is pre-checked nowhere, and it is not required to create an account, sign in, or use min. on the web.
  • We buy no phone lists and import no numbers. Every number min. texts was entered by the person holding that phone, or texted min. first.
  • The code step verifies consent: min. only keeps texting a number once someone has proved they hold that phone.

What min. sends

  • Account and sign-in codes, when you ask for one.
  • Replies to messages you send min.
  • Occasional check-ins about your own calendar, inbox, or to-do list — the things you connected min. to.

Conversational only. No marketing, no promotions, no third-party advertising, and nothing on behalf of anyone else — ever.
