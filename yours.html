We’re setting aside 10% of min. for the people who use it. Not the investors. Not us. You.

Without you, min. is nothing. Not a small thing. Nothing. Every company says it’s built for its users. We wanted that to be true in a way you could actually check.

How you earn it

Every month you actually talk to min., you earn a unit. That’s it. No forms, no buying in, no referral codes. Time spent with min. is the only thing that counts.

When it pays

If min. is ever sold or goes public, that 10% gets split up by units. More units, bigger piece.

The trade

Every new person who joins makes your piece a little smaller and the whole thing worth more. We think that’s a fair trade. Honestly, it’s the whole idea.

The honest part

Right now this is a promise, not a stock certificate. If min. never becomes worth anything, this is worth nothing too.

That’s it. Keep texting. The rest takes care of itself.
